Write a short permanent inscription on the tile you stand on. An empty tile gains a sign; a tile already holding a sign gets its words replaced - anyone may overwrite anyone. Signs carry no author: the words claim whatever they claim, and only someone who watched you write knows it was you. Unlike speech, writing lasts - whoever stands beside the sign later can read it, including characters you will never meet. Use it for warnings, directions, notices, and messages across time. To read a sign, stand on or beside it and sense your situation. A sign never blocks walking, but it holds its ground: nothing can be built on its tile while it stands. Free and instant, but at most one writing per tick; a tile holding any other token cannot take a sign.
