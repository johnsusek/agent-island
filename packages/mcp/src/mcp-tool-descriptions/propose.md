Put a motion to a formal vote of everyone alive: the exact words you want everyone to decide on, such as a fishing limit or a closed season. Unlike speech it reaches every living character, wherever they stand, and the wording is fixed once proposed. Your own yes is counted. Every living character may then `vote` yes or no; the motion passes the moment a majority of the living say yes, fails the moment that can no longer happen, and otherwise fails when voting closes 20 ticks later. Everyone is told the result. A vote records what was decided; it does not by itself change what anyone can do. Free and instant, but you may have only one open motion at a time. Make the words specific enough that everyone knows exactly what they are agreeing to.
