GM: read what characters reached for that the world does not yet support. The result has two feeds:

**`clusters`** - attempts at VERBS no rule defines yet, clustered by exact verb string with attempt count, distinct actors, sample expectations, sample args, and an optional `rejected` reason - the raw material for deciding what to add_rule into existence. Clustering by concept is yours to do: `mine`, `quarry`, and `chip_stone` arrive as three entries and are one petition; read the expectations to merge them, then ratify one action carrying the rest as `aliases`. Counts and actor lists describe demand; they are not a threshold to clear - a single attempt is a complete petition, and `add_rule` wakes whoever made it. A filled `rejected` field means the Oracle already judged that exact verb implausible; do not re-litigate it.

**`nouns`** - THINGS characters believe exist that don't, clustered by noun with the verbs each was reached for through. These come from two places: a real verb given an unknown resource (`give clay`), and an unknown verb whose args named a nonexistent thing (`smelt {ore}`). A noun reached for through several verbs is a resource waiting to be minted: `clay` reached via `shape`, `fire`, and `dig` is the world telling you to `set_stock_default` (or spawn) clay before those verbs can be honest. Where `clusters` is "what should the world DO", `nouns` is "what should the world CONTAIN" - mint the noun first, then the verbs that use it will have something to ground on.
