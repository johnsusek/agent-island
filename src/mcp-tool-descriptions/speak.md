Say one short message out loud. It is heard by whoever stands within earshot - a short conversation range of a few paces around you, not a broadcast; you can see farther than your voice carries, so walk over before you talk - and the result tells you who heard it. Free and instant: costs no time and can be used even while an action is underway. Recent conversation around you is folded into your observation automatically; you are limited to a few messages per tick to keep it from flooding.
